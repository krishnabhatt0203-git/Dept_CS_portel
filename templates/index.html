About us

About the Department

Since 1998, the Department of Computer Science & Engineering has trained engineers who go on to build software, lead research, and start companies. We keep our curriculum close to industry practice while still taking fundamentals seriously.

Our Vision

To be a centre of excellence in computer science education and research, producing engineers who are technically strong and socially responsible.

Our Mission

To deliver an industry-relevant curriculum, encourage undergraduate research, and build strong academia-industry partnerships that create real opportunities for students.
